THE PROCESS

From product data to a launch decision.

LAUNCHGUARD is designed around one moment: deciding whether a product is ready to launch.

1 Add Product

Add your marketplace, category and core product information.

2 Upload Evidence

Add your listing, label, packaging and available compliance documents.

3 Pre-Launch Check

Product, listing and evidence are checked for detectable risks and inconsistencies.

4 Fix Risks

See what should be corrected, verified or supplied before launch.

5 Launch

Make the launch decision with clearer evidence in front of you.

WHY LAUNCHGUARD

Find launch risks before they become Amazon problems.

Compliance problems are often discovered only after Amazon asks for evidence, restricts a listing or triggers enforcement. LAUNCHGUARD moves that check earlier.

Before launch, not after damage.

Look for detectable risks while there is still time to correct them.

More than listing copy.

Compare product information, listing, labels, packaging and available evidence.

No false certainty.

When the available information is not enough, LAUNCHGUARD can return VERIFY or INSUFFICIENT DATA instead of pretending the product is compliant.
